Everything you need

One app for your whole portfolio

From a single flat to a growing portfolio, PropMaster handles the landlord's month without spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads.

Properties

Add each property with its details, see what is tenanted and what is vacant, and open everything about it from one page.

Tenants and leases

Capture the tenant, lease dates, rent, deposit and due day. Invite tenants by email to their own portal.

One-click monthly invoices

Create an invoice for every lease at once: rent plus that month's recoverable costs, due on each lease's due day.

Payments

Built-in statement reader

Upload your COJ statement. PropMaster reads it for you.

Stop typing each line of your municipal account or levy statement. Upload the PDF, a scan or a photo and the charges are read out for you to confirm.

  • Checked against the statement total, so a misread shows up as a warning.
  • Estimated meter readings are flagged before you bill your tenant.
  • You always review first. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Built for South Africa

Not a generic app with rand bolted on
